What is happening inside the line?

Grease does not stay liquid all the way down the line. It cools against the pipe wall, traps scraps and soap, then narrows the path for wastewater. Grease and food waste are commonly named among the materials that contribute to sewer blockages.

How hydro jetting may help

High-pressure water can work around the inside of a suitable pipe rather than only cutting a path through the center of a clog. Equipment may run up to 4,000 PSI, but the setting and nozzle must match the pipe condition. A camera inspection can help locate the buildup and flag cracks or weak sections before cleaning.

A backed-up kitchen sink, gurgling nearby fixtures or another clog soon after snaking are reasons to ask about the main line. Grease collected in a damaged pipe may return unless the defect is repaired. Keeping cooking fat out of drains is still the best way to reduce the next blockage.
